Observability integrations let SuperPlane receive operational signals and collect evidence for investigation, verification and incident response.

## Telemetry and alerts

- [Datadog](/components/datadog)
- [Dash0](/components/dash0)
- [Grafana](/components/grafana)
- [Honeycomb](/components/honeycomb)
- [Logfire](/components/logfire)
- [New Relic](/components/newrelic)
- [Prometheus](/components/prometheus)
- [Sentry](/components/sentry)

## Incident workflows

Use [PagerDuty](/components/pagerduty), [FireHydrant](/components/firehydrant), [incident.io](/components/incident), or [Rootly](/components/rootly) to react to incident events and coordinate follow-up work.

Start with read-only investigation. Require approval before an automation changes production systems.
