---
title: Spending
description: Monitor tracked model and execution cost across a Factory workspace.
---

Use the spending view to connect Factory cost with the tasks and outcomes that produced it. SuperPlane tracks supported model tokens and execution compute; connected providers can charge additional costs separately.

## Review workspace totals

Select a reporting period and compare total cost, completed output, unsuccessful work and retry activity. Open expensive tasks to find the stages and attempts that used the most resources.

## Reduce avoidable spend

Improve intake criteria, limit concurrency, stop unchanged retries and use appropriate models for planning and implementation. A task that needs human judgment should not consume repeated agent attempts.

## Set external controls

Configure budgets and alerts in each AI, cloud and CI provider. SuperPlane cannot enforce charges that the connected provider reports outside its tracked usage.

See [Costs and budgets](/billing/costs-budgets).
